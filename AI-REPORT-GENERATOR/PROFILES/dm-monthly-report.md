# DOMAIN REPORT PROFILE — AHAMOVE DRIVER MANAGEMENT (DM)

This domain profile encodes operational knowledge, core metrics, regional terminology, and preferred narrative structures for **Ahamove Driver Management (DM)** executive reports.

---

## 🎯 CORE OPERATIONAL KPIS & METRICS

### Primary Supply & Performance Metrics:
- **Ontime Rate**: Target $\ge 96.0\%$. Measures SLA fulfillment punctuality.
- **Occupancy Rate**: Driver active working time ratio. Target $65\% – 75\%$.
- **Non-Offload Rate**: Order completion without driver drop/cancel. Target $\ge 98.5\%$.
- **Driver Grade / Tiering**: 4-Tier Driver Hierarchy (Tầng 1 Core/Hub $\rightarrow$ Tầng 4 Casual).
- **Cost per Order (CPO)**: Total Incentive & Bonus Spend / Completed Orders.
- **Acceptance Rate (AR)**: Target $\ge 85\%$. Rate of accepted order dispatches.
- **Fulfillment Rate (FR)**: Target $\ge 92\%$. Net orders successfully delivered.
- **Peak-Hour Supply Surge**: Supply availability during 11:00-13:00 and 17:00-19:00 peak slots.

---

## 🗺️ REGIONAL TERMINOLOGY & BOUNDARIES

- **HCM**: Hồ Chí Minh Hub & Urban Districts.
- **HN**: Hà Nội Capital Hub & Sub-hubs.
- **North**: Hà Nội, Bắc Ninh, Hải Phòng.
- **South**: Hồ Chí Minh, Đồng Nai, Bình Dương.
- **Central**: Đà Nẵng, Huế, Nha Trang.
- **Xmetro**: Suburban / Inter-provincial delivery corridors.
- **Mini-hub**: Local driver support stations & high-density hub nodes.


## 🎨 BRAND IDENTIFIERS & WORDING CONVENTIONS
- **Tone**: Evidence-based, direct (Lead with numbers and P&L impact), action-oriented.
- **Formatting**: Bold key numbers (`96.4%`, `15.8M VND CPO`), use badge indicators (`+1.8% WoW`, `-450 bps`).
- **Terminology**: Use standard logistics Vietnamese-English hybrids (Peak-hour, Mini-hub, Fleet EV, Mega Sales, Onboarding Churn, Driver Moat).
