# VISUAL LAYOUT RULES — 5 CANVAS PRINCIPLES

All generated slides MUST strictly enforce the **5 Gemini/Canvas Visual Layout Principles**:

---

## 1. ASYMMETRIC FOCAL POINT (Điểm Tụ Thị Giác Bất Đối Xứng)
- Never create 3-4 identical, flat vertical columns.
- Always establish **1 Focal Point Element** (e.g. Hero KPI Callout, Heatmap, or Root-Cause Card) that is **1.5x – 2x larger** than surrounding secondary cards.
- Positioning: Place the primary takeaway at top-left, following natural Z-pattern and F-pattern eye scanning paths.

---

## 2. VISUAL FLOW & CONNECTORS (Luồng Dẫn Dắt Thông Tin)
- Structure slide content in a progressive narrative flow:
  `Input / Problem` $\rightarrow$ `Diagnostic Action` $\rightarrow$ `Business Impact`
- Use smooth connectors: step arrows (`➔` or `▼`), numbered badges (`01`, `02`, `03`), or left-to-right grid flow.

---

## 3. FLOATING GLASS CARDS & BREATHABLE MARGINS (Thẻ Nổi & Độ Thở)
- Wrap distinct content blocks inside separate **Floating Cards**.
- Card background MUST contrast against canvas (`#FFFFFF` on `#F8FAFC`).
- **Internal Padding**: Minimum `24px` padding on all sides. No text dangles or touches card borders.
- **Card Spacing**: Maintain `20px - 24px` gutter gaps between adjacent cards.

---

## 4. ICON BADGES & GIANT NUMBERS (Icon Anchor & Con Số Khổng Lồ)
- **Icon Badges**: Every card sub-title MUST start with an emoji icon anchor (e.g. 🎯 Target, ⚡ Impact, 💡 Insight, 🔴 Critical, 📊 Growth).
- **Giant Numbers**: Key performance metrics (e.g. `96.4%`, `45,700`, `+35%`, `-15% SLA`) MUST be extracted into isolated **Giant Callouts** (`48px – 56px Bold/Black`) standing independent of narrative body text.

---

## 5. BALANCED DUAL-PANE COMPOSITION (Cân Bằng 40/60)
- When combining data visualizers/tables with explanatory text cards:
  - **40% area**: Allocated for Visual Data Anchor (Heatmap, KPI Banner, Process Flow).
  - **60% area**: Allocated for Structured Explanation Cards (Insights, Root-Cause, Next Action).
- Never overlay plain text directly over dense charts or imagery. Keep backgrounds clean and high-contrast.
