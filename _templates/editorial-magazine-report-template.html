📐 KPI Formulas & Data Sources
  • Fulfillment Rate (FR %): `(Completed Orders / Total Requests) × 100%`. Target benchmark ≥ 98.5%.
  • Acceptance Rate (AR %): `(Driver Accepted Orders / Total Dispatched Requests) × 100%`. Target ≥ 92%.
  • DQS Score: `(0.4 × AR) + (0.4 × FR) + (0.2 × CSAT)`.
  • Data Grounding: BigQuery Data Warehouse `ahamove-bi-dw.ops_metrics.daily_performance_summary`.
