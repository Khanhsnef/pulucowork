# Chapter 7: Lessons in Storytelling

## Core Idea
Data alone informs, but narrative moves people to action. Frame your visual insights within a structured story arc.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Narrative Arc (Freytag's Pyramid in Business)**:
  1. **Plot / Beginning**: Establish setting, current state, and baseline context.
  2. **Rising Action / Conflict**: Introduce the problem, anomaly, or opportunity.
  3. **Climax**: The pivotal realization or core data insight.
  4. **Falling Action**: The options evaluated and implications.
  5. **Resolution / End**: The explicit recommendation and call to action.
- **Repetition**: Repeat key points across different modalities (Visual, Verbal, Text) to cement long-term memory.
- **Horizontal & Vertical Logic**:
  - *Horizontal Logic*: Reading slide titles alone tells a complete narrative story.
  - *Vertical Logic*: Each slide's content directly supports its title header.
