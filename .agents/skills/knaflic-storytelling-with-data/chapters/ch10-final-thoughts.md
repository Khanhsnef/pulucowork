# Chapter 10: Final Thoughts

## Core Idea
Data visualization is an iterative practice sitting at the intersection of science and art. Upskilling storytelling with data requires continuous practice and organizational culture shift.

## Key Recommendations
1. **Seek Feedback**: Ask colleagues "Where do your eyes land?" and "What is the key takeaway?" to test clarity.
2. **Build an Organizational Style Guide**: Standardize color palettes, chart templates, and typography across teams.
3. **Practice Restraint**: Resist the urge to show all the data you analyzed. Show only what supports the story.
4. **Start Small**: Apply one new principle (e.g. decluttering gridlines or using gray+accent color) to your next presentation.
