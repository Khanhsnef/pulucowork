# Chapter 8: Pulling It All Together

## Core Idea
A complete end-to-end case study demonstrating the transformation of an messy initial data report into a polished, executive-ready data story.

## Case Study Steps
1. **Understand Context**: Identify that leadership needs to decide on funding a customer support initiative.
2. **Choose Visual**: Transition from a crowded table to a clean line chart with forecasted trend.
3. **Declutter**: Eliminate gridlines, heavy borders, redundant decimals, and chart legend.
4. **Focus Attention**: Color the historical trend gray and highlight the crisis drop in bold orange.
5. **Think Like Designer**: Align callout text blocks, use clean sans-serif typography, and establish hierarchy.
6. **Tell Story**: Frame presentation around the risk of inaction vs. financial return of intervention.
