# Chapter 4: Focus Your Audience's Attention

## Core Idea
Leverage how the human brain processes visual information through iconic memory and preattentive attributes to direct visual attention to the most important data point instantly.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Three Types of Memory**:
  - *Iconic Memory*: Super-fast (< 1 second), subconscious processing of visual attributes.
  - *Short-Term Memory*: Limited capacity (3-5 chunks of information).
  - *Long-Term Memory*: Built over time via storytelling and emotion.
- **Preattentive Attributes**:
  - *Form*: Orientation, Shape, Line Length, Line Width, Size, Curvature, Added Marks, Enclosure.
  - *Color*: Hue, Intensity/Saturation.
  - *Spatial Position*: 2D position on the page.
- **Strategic Color Rule**:
  - Use color sparingly and intentionally.
  - Mute 90% of the chart in gray, and use a single high-contrast accent color (e.g. orange or blue) for the key takeaway.
  - Test for colorblindness (ensure red/green aren't the only distinguishing factor).

## The "Where Are Your Eyes Drawn?" Test
Look away from your chart, look back quickly, and note where your eyes land. If they don't land on the main insight, adjust preattentive attributes until they do.
