# Chapter 9: Case Studies

## Core Idea
Practical strategies for resolving tricky data visualization challenges encountered in daily business operations.

## Key Case Scenarios
- **Dark Backgrounds**: Use sparingly. Require high-contrast white/light text and muted pastel accents.
- **Spaghetti Graphs**: When multiple line series cross repeatedly:
  - *Solution A*: Highlight 1 key series in color and mute all others in light gray.
  - *Solution B*: Break into small multiples (panel of small line graphs).
- **Alternatives to Pie Charts**:
  - Replace 100% pie chart with a horizontal bar chart sorted by value.
- **Leveraging Build Animations**:
  - Present data sequentially (step 1 -> step 2 -> step 3) during live presentations to prevent audience from reading ahead.
