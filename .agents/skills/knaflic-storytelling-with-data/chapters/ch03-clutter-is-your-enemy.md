# Chapter 3: Clutter Is Your Enemy!

## Core Idea
Clutter creates unnecessary cognitive load that drains your audience's brainpower. Every element on a chart adds visual noise unless it directly aids comprehension.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Cognitive Load**: The mental effort required to process information. Minimize extraneous cognitive load so full capacity goes to understanding the insight.
- **Gestalt Principles of Visual Perception**:
  1. **Proximity**: Objects close together are perceived as a group.
  2. **Similarity**: Objects sharing color, shape, or size are perceived as related.
  3. **Enclosure**: Objects bound by a border or background shading belong together.
  4. **Closure**: Eyes fill in missing gaps to form recognizable shapes.
  5. **Continuity**: Eyes follow smooth paths and lines rather than sharp angles.
  6. **Connection**: Objects physically connected by a line are perceived as linked.

## Decluttering Step-by-Step Process
1. Remove chart borders and heavy container frames.
2. Remove gridlines or fade them to light gray (`#E5E5E5`).
3. Remove data markers unless highlighting specific outliers.
4. Clean up axis labels (remove trailing zeros, simplify formats).
5. Label data directly instead of using separate legends.
6. Align text and elements consistently (left-align text for readability).
7. Preserve intentional white space around key elements.
